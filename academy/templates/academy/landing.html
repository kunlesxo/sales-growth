Stop buying blind. Learn the process.

Many vendors want to sell these products but don't know how to find suppliers, vet them, pay them, ship goods or judge profit. This masterclass covers each step, so you learn to import smarter.

10 things you will learn

How to source Montessori products directly from China

Identify the products and where and how they are sourced.

How to find and evaluate suppliers

Search major Chinese sourcing and trading platforms, compare suppliers, and check MOQ and supplier details.

How to negotiate better prices

Communicate with suppliers and negotiate by quantity.

How to work around high MOQs

Understand minimum orders, and how group buying makes larger orders possible.

How to set up Alipay and pay suppliers

Link your account, make the payment, and know what to watch for.

What to do when Alipay has issues

Experience-based guidance on verification and restrictions. It does not guarantee approval or account recovery.

How to ship from China to Nigeria

Shipping methods, Chinese warehouses, consolidation, and how to evaluate shipping companies.

How to calculate CBM and your true landed cost

Work out product volume, shipping cost and the total landed cost.

How to calculate your selling price and profit

Decide whether an import is worth it before you commit your money.

How to build and sell the business

Find and sell to parents, schools, crèches, teachers and other resellers.

Seven days, one complete sourcing cycle

Each recorded lesson is built around doing, so you can apply it to your own products as you go.

  1. Day 1

    Foundation and mindset

    Understand the sourcing landscape, set your goals, and prepare your business for importing.

  2. Day 2

    Finding and vetting suppliers

    Search the platforms, spot red flags, and verify suppliers before you send money.

  3. Day 3

    Landed cost and pricing

    Calculate product cost, shipping, duty and your true landed cost, then price for profit.

  4. Day 4

    Ordering and payment

    Negotiate, place orders, pay safely, and track your shipment from factory to door.

  5. Day 5

    Scaling and repeat sourcing

    Build a repeatable system, manage supplier relationships, and grow your catalogue.
